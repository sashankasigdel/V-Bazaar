About Bazaar

Your neighbourhood's digital marketplace.

Our Mission

Bazaar exists to make it effortless for people to find, connect with, and support the local businesses around them — restaurants, cafes, salons, shops, and services — while giving those same businesses a simple, affordable way to reach customers online without needing their own website or app.

What We Offer

  • A searchable directory of local businesses, organized by category and location
  • Business profiles with photos, hours, contact details, and customer reviews
  • Online ordering for businesses that opt in, with order management tools for owners
  • A free, simple way for any business — big or small — to get discovered locally
